Melbourne catchment · baseline v2 · one representative day

One merged stream, two hub sort rounds

Every parcel — catchment pickup, interstate arrivals, locally staged — rides the same stage chain through a shared machine set, and every delivered parcel is now sorted twice at hub level: round 1 at hub A, round 2 at a different hub B (A ≠ B is structural, not a constraint). The 3 sorting pickup PUDs (Bayswater, Melbourne North, Sunshine West) keep exactly 20% of their pickup as round 0 — bag-unloaded, sorted on-site, terminated — and linehaul exactly 80% raw. Delivery PUDs only load — driver sort is removed. Sources = sinks holds across the whole network by construction.

Network flow — merged stream, two hub rounds

Edge numbers = the lane legs in TransportationPolicies; A2–A4 = the stages from the ops architecture slide (A1 = pickup leg, A5 = middle-mile linehauls, now including the 2b hub hop). Unload0/1/2_* and Sort1/2_* have no lanes anywhere; Sort0_* has exactly one controlled lane per sorting PUD — to its own co-located sink. Despatch1_<g>_* only leaves hub g (three hub-pair lanes + g's own interstate sink) and no hub holds the round-2 BOM for its own flavour — so the two sorts always happen at two different hubs, and a parcel can hop hubs exactly once.

The 3 PUD sort sites also hold the local stage (staged volume was sorted on-site the previous day).

Why one stream (what the merge fixed)

  • No duplicated machines. One machine set per sort site serves all volume — fixed cost charged once, both hub rounds competing for the same sorter capacity.
  • No mid-network source/sink. Arrivals enter as raw Pickup_* into the same chain; local pickup flows onward to real delivery — including on-site at the 3 sorting pickup PUDs.
  • Two hub rounds without duplicating hubs. The same 4 hubs play role A and role B; origin-flavoured Despatch1_<hub>_* plus withheld own-flavour BOMs force the two sorts onto two different hubs — no soft constraints, no tuning.
  • One product family. 26 products (9 parcel stages × 2 classes + 4 flavoured Despatch1 × 2), 24 BOMs, 51 work centres, 85 processes, 218 production policies.
